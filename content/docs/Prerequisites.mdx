## Requirements

- A fresh or existing **Arch Linux** install.
- A **normal user** account. Do not run the installer as root.
- An **AMD** GPU. NVIDIA is not supported.
- `git` and `paru`. The installer fetches them if they are missing.

## Who it is for

UmmItOS suits people new to Hyprland as well as heavy Arch Linux users. You should be comfortable with GNU/Linux and the command line.
