# Security

The shell went through a full review and security audit for **v1.0.0-rc2**. The user-facing changes:

## Untrusted text

Text that comes from outside — Wi-Fi names, Bluetooth names, page titles, track names, notifications — is shown as plain text. It can't load remote images, so it can't reveal your IP address.

## Notifications

- Notification bodies keep only bold, italic, underline, line breaks, and real links; anything else is stripped.
- Bodies are cut at 4000 characters, and at most 6 toasts show at once.

## Login screen

The login screen (`greetd`) keeps its cache in `/var/cache/ummitos-greeter`, owned by the greeter user, rather than in `/tmp`.

## IPC

- Setting a wallpaper over IPC only accepts your own wallpapers.
- QR `mailto:`, `tel:`, and `geo:` links are validated before they open.
