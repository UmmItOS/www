# Screenshots and recording

As of **v1.0.0-rc1**, screenshots are handled by the [Quickshell](/docs/configuration/quickshell) shell, which replaced Hyprshot. Captures are copied to the clipboard and saved to disk.

## Keybindings

| Keybinding | Mode |
|------------|------|
| `Print` | Full screen |
| `Shift + Print` | Region |
| `Super + Print` or `Super + Shift + W` | Active window |

`Print` takes the full screen at once, with no selection overlay. Notices stay on screen until the picture is taken, so they appear in it, and the notification panel stays open for the shot.

## Screenshot directory

On the **Screenshots** page in Settings, type a folder in **Save to** and press Enter. It takes effect at once, and an empty field goes back to the default. The choice is saved in `~/.config/ummitos/screenshot.conf`. The same page has an **Open gallery** button (`Super + F`).

<Callout type="info">Use a full path like `~/Pictures`, not a relative one. The field shows a warning if the path is not full.</Callout>

The default is the `HYPRSHOT_DIR` variable in `~/.config/hypr/hyprland/env.lua`, which the installer sets to `~/Pictures`. If it is not set, screenshots go to `~/Pictures/Screenshots`. After editing `env.lua`, log out and back in.

## Gallery

`Super + F` shows every picture in that folder, newest first. Type to search the text inside the pictures (read with tesseract). Enter or a click copies the picture to the clipboard again.

## Screen recording

Press `Super + Shift + R`, choose which sound to record (system, microphone, both or none), and a 3 second countdown starts. Press `Super + Shift + R` again to stop and save. Recording uses `gpu-screen-recorder` through the desktop portal.

<Video
  mp4="/ummitos-record.mp4"
  webm="/ummitos-record.webm"
  poster="/ummitos-record-poster.jpg"
  caption="Super + Shift + R picks the sound (system, mic, both or none), counts 3-2-1, runs a timer on the bar, and Super + Shift + R again stops it. The Record page in Settings sets quality, frame rate and codec."
/>

- The first recording asks which screen to share. Tick **Remember** to skip the question next time.
- The bar shows a timer once recording starts. If you cancel the picker, you get "Recording did not start".
- Files are saved as MP4 in `~/Videos/Recordings` by default.
- The **Record** page in Settings sets the folder, the quality (Small, Balanced or Sharp, which are the high, very high and ultra presets), the frame rate (30, 60, 120, or the screen's own rate, which is the default), the codec (Auto, H.264, HEVC, AV1; HEVC by default), and whether the cursor shows. It also lists your recordings.
