# Desktop Shell (Quickshell)

As of **v1.0.0-rc1**, UmmItOS ships a custom [Quickshell](https://quickshell.org/) desktop shell written in QML. It replaces the old Waybar, Rofi, Swaync, Wlogout, Swww, Hyprlock and Hyprshot stack with a single integrated shell.

## Pages

- [Notifications and alerts](/docs/configuration/notifications): notices and the heavy process warning
- [Desktop and wallpaper](/docs/configuration/wallpaper): the `Alt + W` carousel, the wallpaper drawer, and the desktop menu
- [Displays](/docs/configuration/displays): two screens with `Super + P`
- [Lock screen](/docs/configuration/lock-screen): `Super + L`
- [Screenshots and recording](/docs/configuration/screenshots): pictures and video of the screen
- [Shell tools](/docs/configuration/tools): lecture pen, QR scanner, and other one-key tools
- [Privacy](/docs/Privacy): clipboard history (`Alt + V`) and anti-forensics

## Everything the shell draws

- **Bar**: top panel with workspaces, status, and system info
- **App launcher**: `Super + Return`
- **Dashboard** and **session menu** (`Super + X`)
- **Volume / brightness OSD**
- **Flyouts** for Wi-Fi, Bluetooth, audio, and the accent colour
- **Alt+Tab switcher** and a hot-corner **overview**
- **Health check**: `Super + H` (see [Staying Updated](/docs/Updating))
- **Settings panel**: the gear beside the power button, with pages for Wi-Fi, Bar, Screenshots, Record, Privacy, Anti-forensics, Packages, Update, Language, Misc, and About. The Update page checks for updates and pulls the repo (see [Staying Updated](/docs/Updating)), and the Language page picks English, 廣東話, or 台灣華語
- Copy notices and a charging ripple

The full shortcut list is in [Keybindings](/docs/configuration/keybindings).

## Keyboard

Flyouts, the desktop menu and the Displays chooser work from the keyboard: Up, Down and Tab move a visible focus bar, Return or Space activates. Escape closes a flyout or the desktop menu.

## Accent colour

You can pick the shell's accent colour from its flyout.

## Languages

The **Language** page in Settings offers English, 廣東話 (written Cantonese), and 台灣華語 (Taiwanese Mandarin). Everything the shell draws is translated: the bar, notifications, lock screen, cheat sheet, QR scanner, weather, and dates. The choice switches live and is saved. For Traditional Chinese, `noto-fonts-cjk` is installed.
