# Displays

import { Video } from '@/components/video';

Press `Super + P`, or choose **Displays…** in the desktop menu, to pick this screen only, duplicate, extend, or second screen only. The change applies at once and reverts after 15 seconds unless you keep it.

  mp4="/ummitos-displays.mp4"

  poster="/ummitos-displays-poster.jpg"
  caption="Super+P chooses how two screens work: this screen only, duplicate, extend or second screen only, and reverts after 15 s unless you keep it."
/>

The chooser works from the keyboard: Up, Down and Tab move the focus, Return or Space picks.
