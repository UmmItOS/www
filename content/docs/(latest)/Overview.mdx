UmmItOS is a GNU/Linux distribution based on Arch Linux and Hyprland, with a custom [Quickshell](https://quickshell.org/) desktop shell plus its config and dotfiles.

### Current Status

- **Latest Release**: [**v1.6.0**](https://github.com/UmmItOS/UmmItOS/releases/tag/v1.6.0). Every version is on the [releases page](https://github.com/UmmItOS/UmmItOS/releases) and in the [Changelog](/docs/Changelog).
- **Available**: the terminal installer (`install-menu.sh`) and the dotfiles bundle
- **Languages**: the shell is in English, written Cantonese, and Taiwanese Mandarin
- **Bugs**: please report any issues on the [GitHub repository](https://github.com/UmmItOS/UmmItOS/issues)
